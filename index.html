🤔 What Exactly Is youtube-scout?

youtube-scout is a smart research assistant for anyone who wants to understand YouTube content deeply. You give it one simple topic—like "best gaming mice," "easy vegan recipes," or "personal finance tips for beginners"—and it does the heavy lifting for you.

After a quick search, you receive a professional Excel workbook containing the most relevant and highest-performing YouTube videos, ranked by key metrics. The workbook includes multiple organized sheets so you can instantly see the landscape, pick winning formats, and craft your own content strategy.

⚙️ The Tech Behind It

Built with Python, uses YouTube Data API v3, yt-dlp for transcripts, and openpyxl for creating the Excel workbook. It's designed to work hand-in-hand with Claude Code as a powerful skill, but you don't need any coding skills to use it.

When you use youtube-scout with a topic, you open a single Excel file that's packed with insights. Imagine opening a file with all these tabs:

📊 Videos Sheet

Top ranked videos with title, link, view count, like ratio, comment count, upload date, and a score that shows how well it's performing.

👤 Channels Sheet

Information about the most successful channels covering this topic—subscribers, total views, and recent performance.

📝 Summary Sheet

A leaderboard-style summary with the top 10 video titles, key themes, and a quick overview of what's trending in your niche.

💬 Comments Sheet

Top comments from the leading videos, showing the most popular viewer opinions and feedback about that topic.

📜 Transcripts Sheet

Full text transcripts of the top videos, so you can study exactly how successful creators speak, structure their content, and explain concepts.

🪝 Hooks & Hotspots Sheet

The first 10 seconds of each top video (the hook) and the specific moments in a video where engagement spikes so you understand what keeps people watching longer.
